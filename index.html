About

I am Bo Wang, holding a Ph.D. from the National University of Defense Technology. My research sits at the intersection of multi-frame geometry and 3D motion estimation: given a short video, how much of the scene's motion — dense, long-range, and robust to occlusion — can be recovered, and how far can that carry a machine that has to navigate without GPS?

Along this line I have worked on multi-frame optical flow via feature splatting (IJCV 2024), long-term scene flow estimation for dynamic real-world scenes (T-PAMI 2025), efficient temporal attention for multi-frame flow (IROS 2025), and 3D motion feature splatting for scene flow (T-CSVT 2026). More recently I have been extending the same geometric view to cross-view geo-localization, aligning what a UAV sees with satellite imagery as a substitute for satellite navigation.

Research

  • 01

    Multi-frame Optical Flow

    Fusing several neighbouring frames into a single-resolution, two-frame backbone — through splatting and temporal attention — to handle occlusion without giving up inference speed.

  • 02

    Scene Flow & Long-term 3D Motion

    Recovering dense 3D motion over long sequences of dynamic, non-rigid scenes, with explicit handling of depth noise, spatial occlusion and temporal drift.

  • 03

    Vision-Only UAV Navigation

    Cross-view geo-localization between oblique UAV imagery and satellite tiles, cast as an iterative planar rigid alignment rather than a pure appearance match.

Selected Publications

TPAMI2025

SceneTracker: Long-term Scene Flow Estimation Network

Bo Wang, Jian Li, Yang Yu, Li Liu, Zhenping Sun, Dewen Hu

IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence, 47(9): 8305–8312

Defines long-term scene flow estimation (LSFE) as a task and proposes SceneTracker, a network that tracks 3D motion of a query point across a long frame sequence while resisting 3D spatial occlusion and depth noise. We also build LSFDriving, the first real-world evaluation set for LSFE, covering static backgrounds, moving vehicles, and the joints and garments of moving pedestrians.

IJCV2024

SplatFlow: Learning Multi-frame Optical Flow via Splatting

Bo Wang, Yifan Zhang, Jian Li, Yang Yu, Zhenping Sun, Li Liu, Dewen Hu

International Journal of Computer Vision, 132(8): 3023–3045

A multi-frame optical flow framework built explicitly for single-resolution, iterative two-frame backbones: neighbouring frames are splatted into the reference view so that an off-the-shelf backbone gains multi-frame context at almost no extra cost. Accuracy improves markedly in occluded regions while inference speed stays high, with a 19.4% error reduction over the previous best on Sintel at the time of submission.

T-CSVT2026

SplatFlow3D: Learning Scene Flow via 3D Motion Feature Splatting

Bo Wang, Zhenping Sun, Yang Yu, Jian Li, Li Liu, Dewen Hu

IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems for Video Technology

Couples dense image-plane evidence with sparse depth evidence by hierarchically constructing a 3D motion feature — decoupling first, then re-coupling. The resulting backbone (SimpleFlow3D) is lifted to a weight-sharing multi-frame network, SplatFlow3D, which unifies first-frame and later-frame inference through pseudo inputs and reaches state-of-the-art scene flow accuracy with a minimal parameter budget.

IROS2025

ETA: Learning Optical Flow with Efficient Temporal Attention

Bo Wang et al.

IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)

A pioneering combination of multi-frame input and attention for optical flow. The paper introduces attention confidence, a plug-and-play signal that lets the network consume temporal attention more efficiently — improving accuracy on occlusions while reducing the cost that usually comes with multi-frame settings.

RigidAnchor: Cross-View Geo-Localization for UAV via Iterative Planar Rigid Alignment

Bo Wang, Dekang Zhu, Jian Li, Zhipeng Meng, Bo Wang, Dewen Hu

Localizes a UAV against satellite imagery without GPS by treating the oblique-to-overhead relation as an iterative planar rigid alignment, rather than a direct appearance matching problem. Paper and code are being prepared for release.
